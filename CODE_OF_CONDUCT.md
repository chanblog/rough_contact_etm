# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to providing a respectful, constructive, and welcoming environment for everyone interested in `rough-contact-etm`.

This project is a research-oriented open-source project. Discussions may involve numerical methods, physical modeling assumptions, software implementation, and reproducibility. Disagreement is welcome when it is technical, evidence-based, and respectful.

## Expected behavior

Examples of positive behavior include:

* Using respectful and professional language.
* Giving constructive feedback.
* Explaining technical disagreements clearly.
* Acknowledging uncertainty in numerical or physical interpretations.
* Helping improve documentation, reproducibility, and tests.
* Being patient with new users and contributors.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:

* Harassment, intimidation, or personal attacks.
* Insults, trolling, or deliberately disruptive comments.
* Dismissive or hostile responses to good-faith questions.
* Publishing private information without permission.
* Repeatedly derailing technical discussions.
* Any behavior that makes participation unsafe or unwelcoming.

## Research and technical discussions

This project welcomes rigorous technical criticism. However, criticism should focus on the model, implementation, assumptions, evidence, or documentation, not on individuals.

Examples of acceptable technical criticism:

* “This boundary condition appears inconsistent with the stated assumptions.”
* “This convergence criterion may be too weak for this example.”
* “The electrical model should state whether constriction resistance is pressure-dependent.”

Examples of unacceptable criticism:

* “This is stupid.”
* “You clearly do not understand the topic.”
* “Only an incompetent person would implement it this way.”

Reports will be reviewed as carefully and fairly as possible. The maintainer will respect privacy and will only share information as needed to address the situation.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may take appropriate action in response to unacceptable behavior, including:

* Asking for clarification.
* Requesting edits to comments or pull requests.
* Issuing a warning.
* Temporarily or permanently limiting participation in the project.
* Locking or deleting disruptive discussions when necessary.

The goal is to maintain a productive, respectful, and technically rigorous open-source research environment.
